WealthAxis

A private journal for people who trade and want to know, honestly, how they are doing.

WealthAxis records your trades, spending, net worth, goals and health on your own device. It has no account, no login and no server: nothing you enter leaves your phone unless you export it yourself.

It is a record-keeping tool. It is not investment advice, not a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and not a broker.
