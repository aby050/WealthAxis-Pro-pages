Deleting your data

Last updated 12 September 2026

There is no account to delete. WealthAxis has no sign-up, no login and no server. Every record lives on your own device, so deleting it is entirely in your hands and takes effect immediately.

Delete everything, keep the app

Open the app and go to Settings → Reset all data. This erases every trade, expense, asset, goal, budget and health entry from your device. It cannot be undone, and we hold no copy to restore from, so export a backup first if you want one.

Delete everything, remove the app

Uninstalling WealthAxis removes its storage along with it.

  • iPhone or iPad: press and hold the app icon, then Remove App → Delete App.
  • Android: press and hold the app icon, then Uninstall. To be thorough, go to Settings → Apps → WealthAxis → Storage → Clear storage before uninstalling.

What we hold about you

Nothing. We run no servers, keep no user database, and have never received your records, so there is nothing on our side to request the deletion of. If you previously used an older WealthAxis build that offered a cloud account, write to us and we will confirm removal of anything associated with it.
